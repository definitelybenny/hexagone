About the Game

HexaGone challenges you with deceptively simple hex grid puzzles. Tap a tile to flip it and its neighbors — figure out the right sequence to clear the board. Easy to learn, tough to master.

Features

  • Clean, minimalist hex-grid puzzles
  • Increasingly challenging levels
  • No ads, no timers — just puzzles
